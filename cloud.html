FAQ

Cloud & Servers questions.

Why does the AWS root account need special attention?

The root user can do anything, including closing the account. We enable MFA, remove root access keys, set alternate contacts and move day-to-day work to IAM users and roles.

Can you reduce our cloud bill?

Usually, yes. We look for idle and oversized resources, storage-class savings, data-transfer costs and commitment discounts, then set budgets and alerts so costs stay predictable.

Do you manage servers on Hostinger and DigitalOcean too?

Yes. We manage Hostinger VPS plans and DigitalOcean droplets as well as AWS, Azure and Google Cloud.

How do you know backups actually work?

We schedule regular restore drills to a separate environment, so backups are proven before you ever need them.
