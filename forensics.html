FAQ

Forensics questions.

Will my original device be changed during the examination?

No. We work from verified forensic images made through write-blocking, and record hash values to prove the copy matches the original.

Can you recover data from a damaged or formatted drive?

Often, yes. Logical recovery succeeds in many cases, while physically failed drives may need specialist clean-room work. We assess first and quote before any attempt. Recovery is never guaranteed.

Do you examine mobile phones?

Yes, Android and iOS, depending on device condition and encryption, and only with authorisation from the owner or a competent authority.

Who can request a forensic investigation?

Device owners, organisations investigating their own systems, and legal representatives. Proper authorisation is always documented before we begin.
