Our story

Dependable technology, without the jargon.

The name says it: Cyber Focus. We started CyFocus because too many businesses and families were juggling separate vendors for their website, email, cloud, security and devices, with nobody looking at the whole picture.

Today we cover that whole picture: building and hosting websites, managing cloud and VPS servers, watching for threats with Wazuh, investigating digital evidence, deploying AI, and even helping with books, videos and financial literacy. Whatever the job, the approach is the same: understand first, fix properly, document everything and stay reachable.

  • Rooted in Salem, serving clients across India and abroad.
  • Eight practice areas and 75+ services, one accountable team.
  • Security-first in every website, server and account we manage.
