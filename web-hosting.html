FAQ

Web & Hosting questions.

Can you move my existing website to a new host?

Yes. We plan the migration, copy files and databases, test everything on a staging URL, then switch DNS with minimal or zero downtime.

Do you build with WordPress or custom code?

Both. WordPress suits sites where you edit content often. Hand-coded HTML, CSS and JS suits sites where speed, security and low running costs matter most. We will recommend the right fit.

Will my business email keep working during changes?

Yes. We map your MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records before touching DNS, so mail keeps flowing without interruption.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Monthly care plans cover updates, backups, uptime monitoring, security scans and small content changes.
