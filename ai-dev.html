FAQ

AI & Development questions.

What is vibe coding?

Building software by describing what you want to an AI coding assistant and iterating quickly. We pair that speed with professional review, testing and security hardening.

Can AI run without sending our data to the internet?

Yes. We deploy open-weight models locally on your own GPU workstation or server, so prompts and documents stay inside your network.

What hardware do I need for local AI?

It depends on model size. Many useful models run on a single modern GPU. We will recommend a right-sized machine or a cloud GPU option.

Do you build custom ML models?

Yes, when your problem benefits from one. We start with your data and goals, and use simpler methods where they work just as well.
