Privacy policy

CyFocus (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This website is a static site: it does not use analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies.

What we collect

  • Contact form: the form does not upload anything to a server. When you submit it, your own email app or WhatsApp opens with your message pre-filled. We only receive what you choose to send.
  • Direct contact: if you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your name, contact details and anything you include.
  • Preferences: your light/dark theme choice is stored in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, deliver services you request, and keep records required by law. We never sell or rent your personal data.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the contact page embeds a Google Map. These services may receive your IP address under their own privacy policies. WhatsApp messages are handled under WhatsApp’s terms.
